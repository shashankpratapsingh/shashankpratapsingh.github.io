Teaching philosophy

I have taught College Algebra within Iowa’s large coordinated program four times, which has made me a careful steward of consistency at scale: shared assessments, common standards, and a deliberate eye on equity across sections. Outside the lecture hall, I most enjoy directed reading and reading-group work, where a single hard idea can be turned over slowly until it clicks.
