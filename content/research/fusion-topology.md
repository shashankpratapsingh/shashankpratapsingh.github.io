## Overview

This work unifies Mombelli's algebraic classification of exact module categories with the combinatorial framework of fusion quivers, revealing unexpected geometric structure in the representation theory of Taft algebras.

## Key Question

Can the abstract classification of module categories over pointed Hopf algebras be realized through explicit combinatorial data?

## Approach

By integrating categorical dualization theory with Green ring computations, we establish that:

1. The Fusion Quiver for $\Rep(H_{n,q})$ is the cyclic quiver $Z_n$
2. The internal algebra structure coincides with the McKay quiver of the generating module $M(2,0)$
3. Relations arise naturally from the nilpotency of the quantum generator

## Result

This unification reveals a cylindrical geometry:

$$
\Gamma_s(H_{n,q}) \simeq S^1 \times \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
$$

compatible with the tube components of the Auslander-Reiten quiver, suggesting a unified topological approach to non-semisimple quantum groups.

## Implications

- Provides computational tools for classifying module categories
- Connects representation theory to topological quantum field theory
- Opens pathways for generalizing to other pointed Hopf algebras
