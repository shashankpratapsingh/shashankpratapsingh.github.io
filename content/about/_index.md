I'm a mathematician working at the intersection of algebra, category theory, and mathematical physics. My research focuses on understanding the structure of non-semisimple quantum symmetries through categorical and combinatorial methods.

## Background

Currently investigating the fusion topology of Taft Hopf algebras and their representation categories. This work bridges classical representation theory with modern categorical approaches to quantum groups.

## Interests

Beyond mathematics, I enjoy photography, sketching mathematical diagrams, and exploring the visual aspects of abstract structures. This site serves as a space to share both rigorous research and more exploratory thoughts.
